## Virtual-device boot attempt (Basic v2.7 model)

Both official images booted on the Basic v2.7 QEMU model through the Device Service
session API. Both reached the UIFlow2 banner and then stopped; the captured frame
stayed blank and the serial log did not advance past the banner. Timestamps differ
by milliseconds; the version banner is the only other difference.
