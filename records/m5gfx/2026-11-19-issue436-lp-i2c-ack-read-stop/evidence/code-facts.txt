    i2c: close reads on the LP port with the controller's STOP
    
    A transaction that ends in a read is closed by i2c_stop(), which builds the
    STOP by driving SDA and SCL as normal GPIO. The pads of a low power port are
    routed through the LP IO domain instead, so that STOP never reaches the bus.
    i2c_stop() then resets the controller and every call reports success.
    
    A device therefore takes the next transaction, read or write, as part of the
    one before it. On ToughC5 (ESP32-C5) with a UnitNFC (ST25R3916), whose space B
    access lasts until a STOP, a space A read right after a space B read returned
    0xFF on every attempt. Transactions that end in a write were not affected:
    they are closed by the controller's STOP command, which does reach the pads.
    
    On the LP port, close a read with the controller's STOP as well. The HP ports
    keep the forced stop.
    
    Bus recovery through i2c_stop() still does not reach the LP pads; that is
    left for separate work.
    
    Refs #421
