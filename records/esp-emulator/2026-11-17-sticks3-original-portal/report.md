## Bounded menu-reentry attempt

A fresh run waited a further 320 seconds of wall time after its first-boot controls. The captive-portal framebuffer remained byte-identical throughout the window and the guest emitted 1,944 AP beacons, zero authentication and zero association responses. The original's 300-second setting uses guest milliseconds. This observation did not measure elapsed guest milliseconds, so it neither establishes an original timeout failure nor exercises settings-menu re-entry. The service accepted stop, released the peer, removed mutable Flash, and exited. The nine current peer-focused platform tests also passed.
